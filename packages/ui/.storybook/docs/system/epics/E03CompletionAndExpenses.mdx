# E03 Completion and future expenses

Backlog E03 (#13): E03-S01 complete and reopen tasks and subtasks (#20), E03-S02 see future expenses as one range (#21), E03-S03 see what has no estimate and add one (#47). Spec: `.ai/specs/implemented/2026-11-03-completion-and-future-expenses.md` (implemented: Phase 1 complete and reopen, Phase 2 future expenses, Phase 3 missing estimates). The design decisions below (I1–I4, J1–J10, POSTEP.md) inform its UI/UX. Screen S8.

## Flow

1. Under the heading, one line: "Future expenses 730–950 zł", the sums of the minimums and the maximums of open main tasks with an estimate, each counted once; a task with subtasks counts with their sum (R17). Below it, folded, "2 tasks without an estimate" with "They are not counted in future expenses until they have an estimate."
2. The owner ticks a task's checkbox. Without open subtasks it is completed at once: muted, struck through, and it moves (200 ms) to the end, after the open tasks. The total changes and is read once.
3. With open subtasks, a question first: "Complete “Paint the bathroom ceiling” and its 2 open subtasks?" with "Reopening the task later does not reopen its subtasks." and "Complete task" / "Cancel".
4. Unticking reopens the task: it goes first in the list; its subtasks stay as they were. Completing every subtask does not complete the task.
5. "Add estimate" next to each item without an estimate opens a small form ("Amount" required, "up to" optional, R18); after the last one, focus moves to the total. The items are open tasks without subtasks and open subtasks, a subtask named with its task ("Tiles · Bathroom tiles"); a task with subtasks is never listed itself (D27).
6. A refused change goes back with a message in the row; a failed total shows "Could not load future expenses." with "Try again" in the line, and the list keeps working.

## Components

`TaskCheckbox`, `SubtaskProgress`, `CompleteTaskDialog` (`src/product/tasks/`); `FutureExpenses`, `UnestimatedTasks` (`src/product/expenses/`); built on `Checkbox` styles, `ConfirmDialog` (`confirmVariant="primary"`), `Collapsible`, `Surface`, `FormField`, `Skeleton`.

## Decisions that shape it

- One line, not tiles of numbers; no spent, budget or completed-expenses figure in version 1 (R02.7, P04, D18, D19).
- A subtask's estimate counts through its task's sum, never on its own (R17, R12); a task without an estimate is named, not counted as zero (R10, R13, J9).
- Completed rows stay, muted, after the open ones; a reopened row goes first; display order only (R02.2, decision 2026-10-21).
- Completing asks only when open subtasks would change too; the button is primary, not danger (I2, J3).
- No total yet: "No estimates yet" (J2); the total is a polite status (J4); its error stays in the line (J6).

## Remaining product work

- Completion (#20, Phase 1), future expenses (#21, Phase 2) and missing estimates with "Add estimate" (#47, Phase 3) are implemented. The server returns integer grosze and the unestimated items; the UI formats the amounts (R17).
- The total uses `formatPlnRange`, the same format as a task's estimate (decision F11).
- Completion in the app is optimistic with rollback. The total is refetched after every task change settles; until then it keeps the last server value.
