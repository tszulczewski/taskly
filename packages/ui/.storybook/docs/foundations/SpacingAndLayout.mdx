# Spacing and layout

Spacing follows relations, not pixel values: pick the level that describes how two things relate, and use its token. The gaps grow from the inside out (T7).

## Layout

One content column, centred, at most `layout-content-max-width` (980px). No fixed sidebar: version 1 has two places, the list and future expenses (P01, R01.5). On phones navigation sits at the bottom (P07, R02.11). The gutter is 16px up to 640px and 24px above (T7); `wide:` is the only breakpoint utility.

<Unstyled>
  <Story of={Spacing.Widths} />
</Unstyled>

The stories **Column 320**, **Column 768** and **Column 1440** in the sidebar render the column at those widths.

| Rule                                                                   | Source                                  |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------- |
| Touch targets are at least 44 × 44px on phones (`touch-target-min`).   | T10, Apple HIG; above WCAG 2.5.8 (24px) |
| A task row is at least 64px high: two lines with 12px above and below. | Luki 6, T10                             |
| Subtasks are indented by 24px per level.                               | R02.1, T10                              |
| No horizontal scrolling at 320px.                                      | WCAG 1.4.10                             |
