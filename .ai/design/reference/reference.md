## R01
Source: .ai/design/reference/R01-linear.png
I like: simple, clean and minimalistic design without unnecesary elements like shadows, gradients, or textures. The use of whitespace and clear typography makes the design easy to read and navigate. Not every element have to be a button, and the use of color is limited to a few key elements, which helps to create a cohesive and visually appealing design. Minimum amout of separated windows and panels.

Why this suits our product: Inspire for simple and clean represenation of the tasks etc. The design is focused on the content and functionality, rather than unnecessary visual elements. It also allows for easy navigation and understanding of the product's features.
How strongly: only inspiration
