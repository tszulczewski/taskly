About this prototype

This page simulates version 1 of the task list for one person. All tasks are fictitious and live only in this page's memory.

Not part of this prototype

  • Real cost, payments, incurred expenses and expenses of completed tasks (version 2)
  • AI estimates
  • Effort or amount of work
  • Marking which job must come before which
  • Shopping list
  • Sharing with other people
  • Signing in and out
  • Creating or editing places (a fixed sample set is used)
  • Drag and drop (use Move up and Move down)
