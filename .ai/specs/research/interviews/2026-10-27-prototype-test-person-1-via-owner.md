# Interview — test participant 1, future owner, prototype test, 2026-10-27 (second-hand, relayed by the owner)

Captured in a conversation with the owner after a test with real people. The participant was not interviewed directly; the owner relayed what they observed. Wording is the owner's retelling in Polish, summarised in English here. The participant is a future owner (not yet living in the property) (owner, 2026-10-27), the same person as the future owner of `2026-10-15-interview_future_owner_of_the_house.md` (owner, 2026-10-27), so this note adds an observation of a known participant, not a new participant. The test used prototype revision 002 (`.ai/prototypes/discovery/task-list-v1/revision-002/`). The device and the length of the session were not stated.

- Recent situation or task the person described: entered their own renovation jobs into the Taskly discovery prototype (revision 002).
- What they did, step by step (as relayed):
  - Where a rough cost was known, typed it as the task's estimate.
  - Where the exact price was known, typed the same number into Min and Max.
  - Tasks without a known cost were filled in mainly with subtasks rather than an amount.
  - For simple (not composite) tasks, sometimes typed an amount.
  - Smaller tasks were left without an estimate.
- What worked, what did not, and any consequences they described: typing one exact price meant entering the same number twice. The owner said "nie dużo" about the small unestimated tasks; whether that means few tasks were left unestimated or that the amounts were small is unclear.
- Other approaches they tried and how those worked: not described.
- Reordering (as relayed, for the test as a whole; the owner did not say which participant): moving tasks was awkward because the prototype had only Move up / Move down and no drag and drop, which was hard with many subtasks.
- The outcome they were trying to achieve and what happened: not described beyond the steps above.
- Verbatim quotes worth keeping (mark each as quote): none; all relayed.
- What they explicitly did not care about: not described.
