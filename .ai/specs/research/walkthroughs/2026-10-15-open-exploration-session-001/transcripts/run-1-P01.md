# Transcript run 1 P01

## Q1. The last time you had to work out what to do next about the flat

**Question**: Tell me about the last time you had to work out what to do next about the flat. What did you do, step by step?

**Passages attached**: "Chcemy mieć plan co zrobić najpierw, a co później..." and "Chciałem zrobić to w excelu ale zrobil sie chaos, mam mniej wiecej zapisane co musimy zrobić z takich ważneijszych prac, mam szacunkowe koszta".

**Persona lines used**: first-time buyer, flat in developer state (bare plaster); wants a plan of what first and what later; tried Excel and it became chaos; has rough cost estimates for the main works; not a computer person.

**Fast reaction**: Ugh, again that. I sat down to put it in order and it just got away from me.

**Feeling**: anxiety, strong, trigger: not knowing where to start and the fear of a wrong order.

**Considered answer**: I wrote down the main works we have to do and next to them rough costs, what I have an idea about. I wanted to put it in Excel and see the order. But it became chaos, I did not know what goes first and what later. So I have the list and rough costs, but no plan. I am not a computer person, so I stopped.

**Adversary line**: And here is why I would still not use something like this: I already have a list and I am tired of starting new lists that become chaos again.

## Q5. Last time you wrote down jobs and costs

**Question**: Last time you wrote down your jobs and costs somewhere, what happened to that list?

**Passages attached**: Excel "chaos"; at work the machine-time app is "bardzo wygodne, kończę na danej koparce i zaznaczam że zwalniam sprzęt i mam też czas pracy".

**Persona lines used**: works full time operating construction machinery; the work app is very convenient; phone apps only for contact and entertainment.

**Fast reaction**: It sits there in chaos. I don't go back to it.

**Feeling**: frustration, moderate, trigger: effort that ended in a mess.

**Considered answer**: The list exists but it became chaos and I have not kept it in order. At work the app for machine time is very convenient: I finish on a machine, mark that I release it, and I have the work time too. That is simple, two taps. I would want something like that, not a spreadsheet.

**Adversary line**: And here is why I would still not use something like this: at work the app is needed for the job, at home nobody makes me open it.
