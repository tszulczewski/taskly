# Walkthrough: open exploration, product-brief.md, 2026-10-15

The most consequential repeated hypothesis: every persona who had the problem would raise the **time or amount of work** per job and **which job must come before which**, while the brief defers effort (D05) and leaves order to manual reordering. The real check is to ask real renovators what they would need to see to pick the next job. Every finding below is synthetic and does not satisfy the Definition of Ready. Replication here is two voices seen twice: both runs were built from the same two interview notes, so a repeated finding shows what those two notes contain, not what a wider group would say.

## 🎯 Scope and limits

Open exploratory interviews on the brief; no flow, no screens. Each persona was also put under three pressures against a neutral description of the first part (subtasks and costs typed by the user, important and urgent markers, rooms, reordering, recorded payments, totals; no estimates; price unstated). Twenty minutes and fifteen jobs were pressure parameters set by the interviewer, not persona numbers. The owner's two own notes were not used, to keep the brief's author out of the panel. Homogeneity flag: four engaged personas use near-identical words ("simple and quick", "intuitive", Excel became chaos); the flag is itself a finding about the material (two voices only).

## 🔍 Barriers that repeated (worst first, ties marked)

### 1. Nothing useful appears until a lot has been typed in, so the persona stops.

[SYNTHETIC]

Weight: 6; spread: ±0; personas: P01, P02, P03 (run 1), P04, P05, P06 (run 2).
Under the "fifteen jobs before anything shows" pressure all six close it or never start; P01 and P02 say they would feel the same as with Excel. Only the unhurried weekend condition leads the engaged personas (P01, P02, P04, P05) to enter everything. The two indifferent seats (P03, P06) only look once.
Touches D04 (costs entered by the user) and A02. Real check: recruit renovators in both segments, give them their own list of about as many jobs as they have, and observe what they enter in the first sitting and whether they return; compare with a start that shows a total after the first job.

### 2. They may not trust, or may leave gaps in, numbers they type themselves. Tied with 3, 4 and 5.

[SYNTHETIC]

Weight: 4; spread: ±0; personas: P01, P02 (run 1), P04, P05 (run 2).
P01: not sure the typed costs are right; P04: would not base a loan on numbers typed by themselves; P02: still guessing the hard part; P05: would forget a tool they need, since they type everything. Touches A02 and D04. Real check: ask renovators how they arrived at their last cost figure and how much they relied on it; test whether entered figures are later corrected.

### 3. Fear of one more list that ends up abandoned like the others. Tied with 2, 4 and 5.

[SYNTHETIC]

Weight: 4; spread: ±0; personas: P01, P02 (run 1), P04, P05 (run 2).
Each already has an Excel list, notes, or paper; switching means retyping, and none would move the whole old list in. Touches A01 (does it change how they order tasks). Real check: ask what happened to their last list and how long it stayed current; observe whether anyone migrates an existing list.

## 📋 Missing cases that repeated

**Which job must come before which (order and blocking).**

[SYNTHETIC]

Weight 4; spread ±0; P01, P02 (run 1), P04, P05 (run 2). P01 and P04 describe advice about designing the kitchen early so walls aren't chiselled twice and say a plain list would not have told them. P02 and P05 ask whether a job is worth doing before or after another. The brief's Now has manual reordering and important/urgent markers only, and N03 excludes the benchmark's wider MVP, which included dependencies. Real check: ask what they would need to see to pick the order; test whether manual reordering is enough. Tied with the others at weight 4.

**Time or amount of work per job.**

[SYNTHETIC]

Weight 4; spread ±0; P01, P02 (run 1), P04, P05 (run 2): "how long it takes", "how much work", "ile czasu zajmie". The owner said effort can be dropped for now (D05); the notes the panel was built from ask for it, so this is a possible contradiction with D05. Real check: ask each segment which of cost, time and work they check first when choosing a job, and what they do without it.

**Money available this month decides what is done.**

[SYNTHETIC]

Weight 2; spread ±0; P02 (run 1), P05 (run 2): they finish step by step from what remains of the monthly pay and put jobs off when too expensive. D06 says the app shows costs and does not store money to spend. Real check: ask where they keep the month's available money today and how they compare it with job costs.

**Missing materials and tools that add cost after a job starts.**

[SYNTHETIC]

Weight 2; spread ±0; P02 (run 1), P05 (run 2). Real check: ask about the last job that needed something not planned and how it was handled; test whether subtask costs capture materials and tools.

**A view of the necessary minimum for a loan decision.**

[SYNTHETIC]

Weight 2; spread ±0; P01 (run 1), P04 (run 2), both future owners. Not in the brief. Real check: ask future owners how they decide whether to borrow and what figure they need.

**Two-tap capture like the machine-time app at work.**

[SYNTHETIC]

Weight 2; spread ±0; P01 (run 1), P04 (run 2). Real check: observe how long a first entry takes and ask what they liked in the work app.

## ⚠️ Contradictions with the brief

- Time or amount of work (above) against D05: replication 2 of 2 runs, personas listed there.
- Money available this month (above) against D06: replication 2 of 2 runs.

## 🔍 Outliers

P03 and P06, the indifferent seats, would not open it after work and would not start the fifteen-job entry; with an open weekend they would look once and give no reason to return (both runs). They are assumption personas for salience, so this says only that the panel includes someone who sees no problem, which fits the idea that part of the market has no need.

## 🔁 Seen once, not reported as a finding

- Same-evening cleaning of dirty kitchen work shapes what can be done in an evening (P02, run 1).
- A wish not to set up something for more than one person (P02, run 1) and a worry that the partner will not look at it (P05, run 2): both flagged as going beyond the persona lines; sharing is not a finding of this panel.
- Breaking big jobs into parts to estimate them (P04, run 2); relevant to subtasks.
- At home nobody makes them open an app, unlike at work (P01, run 1).
