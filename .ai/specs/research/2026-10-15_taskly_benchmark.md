# Taskly — Competitor Summary and Product Takeaways

**One-page brief · Polish market + selected foreign apps · analysis as of 15 October 2026**

> **Thesis:** a task list + shopping + budget is no longer unique. Taskly's opportunity is the combination: **plan the work → calculate materials and cost → buy → settle up**, with a credible AI cost estimate for the Polish market.

## 1. Market comparison

| App | Main advantage / model | Takeaway for Taskly |
|---|---|---|
| **Remontowy Planer** | Closest Polish benchmark: schedule, budget, shopping and rooms. Freemium; approx. PLN 9.99–19.99/month. OCR is paid, AI is described as planned. | The plan + shopping + budget set alone is already on the market. |
| **Pilt** | Tasks, team, chat, budget and an AI assistant; freemium + Pro. Price varies by channel/region. | Competes in work organization and collaboration; per-task AI cost estimation not confirmed. |
| **Budget My Reno** | Expense control, documents, sharing and AI for invoices. Free for 1 renovation; approx. USD 12.99 lifetime or USD 1.99/month. | Strong financial competitor. |
| **Buildry** | Rooms, work history, planned/actual costs and materials linked to rooms. Freemium + one-time payments. | Worth comparing work tied to rooms and materials. |
| **Scope / RenoBudget / RenoTracker** | Budget, ordered and spent costs, quotes, receipts and documents; freemium models and paid plans. | Cost control is a crowded area. |
| **HomeZada / Houzz Pro** | Broader tools for homeowners or professionals. Reviews mention the benefits of integration, but also cost and complexity. | Don't compete on system size; win on simplicity of day-to-day work. |

**Signals from reviews:** for many new apps there are too few ratings to draw firm conclusions. In more mature tools, users appreciate integration, but the same risks recur: complexity, manual data entry and price. These are hypotheses to test, not a representative study.

**Gap to verify:** the descriptions reviewed did not confirm the full combination: *AI cost estimate for a specific job → automatic materials list → settling the actual cost*. This is a promising differentiator, but it needs validation.

## 2. Takeaways for Taskly

**Positioning:** *Taskly turns a renovation plan into concrete purchases and a realistic financial plan.*

- **AI cost estimate for a specific task:** low / typical / high range, labor and materials separately, explicit assumptions, price date and region. Prices should come from an updated source.
- **Shopping linked to the work:** suggested materials, quantities and units; a change in scope signals that the list needs updating.
- **Budget in 3 layers:** forecast, commitments (quotes/orders) and actual spending — without double counting.
- **Simple collaboration:** quick adding, rooms, responsible people, task dependencies and permissions for a partner/contractor.

### MVP — build first

1. Projects, rooms, tasks and subtasks; one task can span multiple rooms.
2. Drag and drop, basic dependencies, filtering, sharing and ownership.
3. AI estimation, a linked materials list, actual cost and a clear budget summary.

### Monetization to test

Freemium for one project + a one-time fee for an extended renovation pack or AI features; subscription for multiple projects and frequent use. Test willingness to pay for the cost estimate, sharing and the planner itself separately.

### Risks and next step

**Risks:** inaccurate AI prices, too much manual entry and double counting of materials. **Next step:** test a clickable prototype with 10–15 people during or after a renovation. Measure handling time, the scale of estimate corrections and willingness to pay.
