## Peopel problem with interior and exterior finishing projects
- **Problem:** People often struggle with estimating the costs of tasks, materials, and overall budget for their home improvement projects. This can lead to overspending, delays, and frustration.
- **Needs:** Users need a reliable tool that can provide accurate cost estimates, generate materials lists, and help them manage their budget effectively. They also need a simple and intuitive interface that allows for easy collaboration with contractors or family members involved in the project. 
- **Solution:** A web application that combines task planning, AI cost estimation, automatic materials list generation, and real-time budget tracking. The app should be user-friendly, allowing users to easily input project details, receive accurate estimates, and manage their budget without confusion or double counting.
